# Global elections: land vs people (v2)

Every election drawn two ways: where the land is, and where the voters are. Each country page opens with a short briefing (who is elected, how the voting system works, what the result means at home and abroad, and what the other result would have meant), then the map.

## Layout
- `index.html` — global gallery (generated by `python3 src/site_gallery.py` from `gallery.countries.json`)
- `usa/` — midterms hub (`python3 src/usa_hub.py`), 54 race pages in `usa/elections/`, 2024 national page in `usa/2024/`
- `new-zealand/` — Lens-engine page (plus `maori/` layer)
- `brazil/`, `bulgaria/`, `trinidad-tobago/` — tap-to-morph pages (own map and data, kept as is)
- `content/briefings/*.json` — briefing text per election, as of 10 November 2026
- `src/briefing/` — briefing renderer; `python3 src/briefing/inject.py` (re)applies nav, typography and briefing to the tap-to-morph pages and the US hub. Idempotent.

## Adding a country briefing
Copy a JSON in `content/briefings/`. Required: `stats`, `elects`, `system{oneline,steps,more}`, `home`, `world`, `otherway{h,p}`, `sources`, `asof`. Optional visual: `hemicycle` or `bar`, and `table`. Add the page to `PAGES` in `inject.py` and a card to `gallery.countries.json`.

## Known limits
- Brazil, Bulgaria, Trinidad and Tobago and USA 2024 keep their tap-to-morph maps; they have not been rebuilt on the Lens engine.
- Build artifacts (`warp-rings`, `lens-fields`, mp4) are not committed for the 54 US races; rebuild with the engine pipeline (1–3 minutes each).
- NZ voting-system and "other way" sections are patched into the built pages and `src/page/nz_intro_body.html` (see `src/page/nz_add_sections.py`).
- Figures are provisional: US counts and NZ special votes were still moving.
